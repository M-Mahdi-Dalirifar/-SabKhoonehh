# Code of Conduct

SabKhooneh welcomes respectful, constructive participation.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful in Issues, pull requests, reviews, and discussions.
- Focus criticism on code, design, documentation, and behavior rather than people.
- Give contributors enough context to understand requested changes.
- Respect privacy. Do not post personal household information, credentials, or private conversations.
- Assume good intent while still addressing technical and security concerns clearly.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sexual harassment, doxxing, deliberate disruption, or repeated hostile behavior are not accepted.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainer at mohammadmahdidalirifar@gmail.com. Reports will be reviewed based on the available evidence and project access may be restricted when needed to protect contributors.

This policy applies to project spaces and project-related communication.
